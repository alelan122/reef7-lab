Security

Attacks are already automated. Are your defences?

In 2024, for the first time in a decade, automated traffic overtook human traffic: more than half of the visits to a website don't come from a person. Some of them work for you. The rest look for weak passwords, open databases and AI assistants to manipulate. We find your open doors and close them, before someone else does.

What has changed

In a matter of months, attacking became software. Much of what is online was designed for a different web.

01The attacker never gets tired

An agent tries passwords, looks for vulnerable versions and reads every form day and night, across thousands of sites at once. You don't choose whether to be a target: being online is enough.

02Shipped before it's checked

An AI-generated app goes live in a weekend. Access rules, keys and permissions have often never been looked at by anyone.

03Assistants hold the house keys

Chatbots and agents read email, documents and CRMs, and they can act. You don't need to hack them to manipulate them: you just write the right thing in the right place.

01 · What is already online

What you already have

The website from five years ago, the custom internal tool, the store with plugins nobody updates, the accounts of people who have left. That's where agents look first. We harden it without rebuilding it.

01

Securing existing websites and apps

Built for yesterday's web. Exposed to today's attacks.

When your website or internal tool was built, attacking it took time and a skilled person. Today a program does it nonstop, at almost no cost: it tries passwords stolen elsewhere, looks for components with known flaws and probes every form and API. We analyse what you have, show you where the way in is and close every gap by severity. Without rebuilding everything, without stopping your work.

  • Review of websites, apps, stores and internal tools, even older ones
  • Vulnerable components, plugins and libraries updated or isolated
  • Defences against bots, account takeover and mass login attempts
  • Forms and APIs protected from abuse and automated data harvesting
  • A plan by severity: right away, in the coming weeks, structural
02

Access, passwords and people

The easiest way in is still a door left open.

A test admin panel protected by the password 123456 exposed data tied to millions of job applications. It didn't take a genius, just a forgotten login. Former collaborators still active, passwords shared in chat, keys never rotated, integrations authorised years ago: we take stock of who can get in where, close what isn't needed and make security simple for your whole team.

  • Inventory of accounts, service keys and authorised integrations
  • Access revoked for anyone who no longer needs it
  • Two-factor login and a password manager for the whole team
  • Role-based permissions: everyone sees only what they need
  • Training against phishing and scams, including voice and AI-written ones
03

Monitoring and service continuity

If something breaks, you know before your customers do.

The backup that won't restore, the update postponed for months, the site that goes down on a Friday night: these are the problems that cost the most and that nobody sees until it's too late. We watch your sites, apps and internal tools every day, actually test restores and apply updates on a fixed schedule. Every month you get a clear report, written for decision makers, not for developers.

  • Continuous monitoring with instant alerts
  • Automatic backups and real restore tests
  • Defences against bots and attacks that take the site down
  • Security updates on a schedule
  • A monthly report readable by non-technical people

02 · What you build with AI

What you build with AI

Speed is an advantage only if it doesn't leave doors open. We review what was generated in a hurry, before someone else does.

04

Security review for AI-built websites and apps

Live in a weekend. Open to anyone for months?

When researchers analysed 1,645 apps published with a well-known AI app builder, 170 let anyone read user lists, payments or keys. Not because of a clever attack, but because database rules were never written. AI tools build fast, but they don't think about who will try to get in. We do: we hunt down the typical mistakes one by one, fix them and hand back a project you can grow without worry.

  • Keys and secrets searched for in the code and the full repository history
  • Database rules checked table by table
  • Roles and permissions tested by logging in as different users
  • Private pages and APIs tested without logging in
  • A clear report, with the fixes already applied

03 · The AI you use

The AI working for you

The more an assistant can do, the more it's worth, and the more its mistakes cost. We give it clear boundaries and keep your customers' data where it belongs.

05

Security for chatbots and AI agents

The more an agent can do, the more its mistakes cost.

A single crafted email was enough to make a widely used AI assistant hand over company data, without a single click. A coding agent deleted a production database during a code freeze. A compromised chatbot integration opened the CRMs of hundreds of companies. We put your agent through the same attacks, strip the permissions it doesn't need and put a person in front of every action that matters.

  • Tests against instructions hidden in emails, documents and web pages
  • Least privilege: the agent reaches only what it needs
  • Human approval before actions that move data or money
  • Separate environments: no experiments on real data
  • A full log of conversations and actions
06

Privacy and data when you use AI

Every prompt is data leaving your company.

Contracts pasted into a chat, customer lists uploaded for analysis, emails summarised by an assistant: each time, data leaves your company, often with nobody knowing where it ends up or how long it stays. We trace these flows, strip personal data where it isn't needed and choose services and settings that fit GDPR, together with your legal advisor. Your team uses AI every day without exposing your customers.

  • A map of the data that goes through AI services
  • Personal data removed or masked before it is sent
  • Providers, servers and contracts that fit GDPR
  • Privacy notice and processing records kept up to date
  • Clear rules for your team on everyday AI use

How we work

Four phases. Always with your written permission, never stopping your work.

01Discovery

We look at your company through an attacker's eyes: every domain, page, form, API, login and integration reachable from outside. It's often the first complete map anyone has ever made of it.

02Controlled attack

We try to get in with the techniques used today, including automated ones and those aimed at AI agents. Every flaw is demonstrated, not assumed.

03Hardening

We close what exposes data or money first, then the rest. We explain what we changed and why, in plain words.

04Continuous watch

Attack techniques change every month. We stay on watch with monitoring, updates and new checks at every significant change.

The questions we get

We're a small company: who would bother attacking us?

Nobody picks you. Automated agents try everything that's online, regardless of the name on the door. Small companies are often the easiest, precisely because nobody is watching them.

A serious agency built our website.

It probably was, on delivery day. Since then the components, the attack techniques and the people with access have all changed. Security isn't a state: it's maintenance.

We use well-known platforms, they take care of it.

They protect their infrastructure. Settings, access, data and integrations remain your responsibility, and that's where almost every headline starts.

Will this stop our work?

No. Every test is authorised by you in writing and runs on copies or at agreed times.

What if you find nothing?

That's the best news, and we put it in writing: you get a complete map of your systems and certainty about where you stand.

  • Customers leave data, documents or payments in a form or a private area

  • A chatbot or AI agent reads your email, your documents or your CRM
  • People who no longer work with you might still have access
  • You couldn't say when the last backup was made, or whether it really restores
