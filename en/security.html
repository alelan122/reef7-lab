Security

The web is full of agents. Not all of them work for you.

Automated programs and AI agents visit websites, apps and internal tools every day. Some bring customers, others look for a way in. We help you find the doors left open and close them, before someone else finds them.

What has changed

The rules changed fast, and much of what is online was designed for a different web.

01Attacks never sleep

What used to take a person hours of work is now done by a program, thousands of times a minute across thousands of sites at once. You don't need to be an important target: being reachable is enough.

02We build faster than we check

With AI a website or app can go live in days. It works, but often nobody has checked who can read the data, what is left exposed in the code and what happens when someone forces a form.

03AI assistants hold the keys

Chatbots and agents connected to email, documents and internal systems can read and act. One instruction hidden in the right place is enough to make them do what they shouldn't.

01 · What is already online

Existing sites and apps

The website from a few years ago, the custom internal tool, the store with plugins never updated, the logins nobody remembers. We make them solid without rebuilding them.

01

Securing existing websites and apps

Built for a web without agents. Secured for the one we have now.

Most of the websites, apps and internal tools in use today were built when attacking them took time and skill. Now automated programs do it nonstop: they try passwords, look for versions with known flaws and probe every form and API. We start from what you have, find the weak points and close them one by one, without rebuilding everything from scratch.

  • A full review of the sites, apps and tools you use, even older ones
  • Outdated components and plugins updated or isolated
  • Defences against bots and mass login attempts
  • Forms and APIs protected from abuse and automated data harvesting
  • A plan by severity: right away, in the coming weeks, structural
02

Access, passwords and people

The most common way in is still a forgotten account.

Former collaborators with active logins, passwords shared in chat, service keys never rotated: many breaches don't start from a technical flaw but from access that was never closed. We take stock of who can get in where, remove what isn't needed and make it easy for your team to work securely.

  • An inventory of accounts, access and service keys
  • Access removed for anyone who no longer needs it
  • Two-factor login and a password manager for the whole team
  • Role-based permissions: everyone sees only what they need
  • Short training against phishing and scams, including AI-written ones
03

Monitoring and service continuity

If something stops, we know before your customers do.

A site down for a day, a database lost without a copy, an update postponed for months: these are the problems that cost the most and only show up when it's too late. We keep watch over your sites, apps and internal tools, check that backups can really be restored and apply updates on a fixed schedule.

  • Continuous monitoring with instant alerts
  • Automatic backups and regular restore tests
  • Protection from bots, spam and attacks that overload the site
  • Security updates on a schedule
  • A monthly report, clear even for non-technical readers

02 · What you build with AI

Built with AI

Speed and security can coexist. We review what was generated in a hurry, before it becomes a problem.

04

Security review for AI-built websites and apps

Online in days. Checked before someone else checks it for you.

AI tools that write code build fast, but they don't think about who will try to get in. The mistakes are nearly always the same: secret keys in visible code, databases open to anyone, private pages reachable without logging in, permissions enforced only in the interface. We hunt them down one by one, fix them and hand back a project you can grow with confidence.

  • Keys and secrets searched for in the code and the repository history
  • Database rules checked table by table
  • Roles and permissions tested by logging in as different users
  • Forms and APIs protected from abuse and bots
  • A clear report, with the fixes already applied

03 · The AI you use

Agents and data

AI assistants deliver the most when they can act. We make sure they act only as intended, and that your data stays where it belongs.

05

Security for chatbots and AI agents

An assistant with the house keys must know which doors to keep shut.

An agent that reads email, searches documents and updates your systems is useful precisely because it can act. For the same reason it can be manipulated: an instruction hidden in a message, an attachment or a web page can push it to reveal data or take actions nobody asked for. We put it through real attacks and give it precise boundaries.

  • Tests against hidden instructions and manipulation attempts
  • Every user reaches only their own data, even through the agent
  • Human approval before any action that moves data or money
  • Usage and spending limits
  • A log of conversations and actions, available at any time
06

Privacy and data when you use AI

Use AI without handing over your customers' data.

Every piece of text sent to an AI service carries the data inside it. We trace what leaves your company, to which providers and how long it is kept; we strip personal data where it isn't needed and choose services and settings that fit GDPR, together with your legal advisor.

  • A map of the data that goes through AI services
  • Personal data removed or masked before it is sent
  • Providers, servers and contracts that fit GDPR
  • Privacy notice and processing records kept up to date
  • Clear rules for your team on everyday AI use

How we work

Four phases, always with your permission and without stopping your team's work.

01Discovery

We map everything reachable from the outside: domains, pages, forms, APIs, logins and connected services. It's often the first time anyone has seen the full picture.

02Controlled test

We try to get in the way an attacker would, with your permission. Every flaw we find is documented and demonstrated, without alarmism.

03Hardening

We close the gaps by severity, starting with those that expose data or money, and explain what we changed and why.

04Monitoring

Risks change every week. We stay on watch with monitoring, updates and new checks whenever something changes.

This is for you if

  • Your website or app is a few years old and nobody has checked it since
  • Customers leave data in a form, a private area or an online store
  • Your site or app was built with AI tools
  • You use a chatbot or AI agent connected to email, documents or internal systems
