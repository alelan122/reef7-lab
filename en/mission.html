Mission

One project at a time, for the sea.

Our name comes from the reef, and reefs are in danger: since the 1950s, the world's living coral cover has halved. That's why we want our work to help the people who protect marine flora and fauna every day.

How it will work

01Our goal

We want every website, app or system we build to help protect the sea.

02One reef at a time

We'll focus on one reef at a time: once a goal is reached, we'll move to the next.

03Always transparent

We'll share every step forward here, reef by reef.

Our story

For us, the sea isn't a slogan.

Before founding Reef 7 Lab, Alessandro spent several years as an active member of 2hands Ancona, the volunteer group that cleans beaches and coastlines of litter.

Today he wants to bring that same commitment to Reef 7 Lab's work: making sure the projects we build help protect the sea we're named after.
