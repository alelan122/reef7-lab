Questions

If yours isn't here, just ask.

What's the free AI audit?

A 30-minute call where we look at how your company works and where AI could save you time. You leave with two or three concrete ideas, even if we never work together.

Why is the Discovery paid?

Because it's real work: you end up with a prototype, a plan and a fixed quote that are yours to keep. If we go ahead, the €490 is credited toward the project.

How does payment work?

Usually 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Longer projects are split into milestones.

What if the project runs late?

If I miss the agreed delivery date, you get 10% off. We set the dates together after the Discovery.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. At handover you get the code, access and documentation, and the data stays in your accounts.
