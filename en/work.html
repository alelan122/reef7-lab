Case study

83% lower software costs. A team 40 times faster.

Asia Baldoni Fitness is a seven-figure online fitness company, with around twenty people across sales, coaching, marketing and operations. It had grown fast, and to keep up it had come to rely on 54 different tools. Together with Tartufoli Group we replaced them with a single platform, built around the way they work.

Online fitness, seven-figure revenue

The problem: growth kept getting more expensive

Every new need brought a new subscription. One CRM for sales, another tool for email, one for landing pages, one for calendars, and yet more tools just to make them talk to each other. By mid-2026 software spend had reached 6.3% of revenue, more than twice the target.

Cost was only the visible part. The hidden part was time: every lead copied from one system to another, every automation built by hand, every hiccup landing on one person's desk. And with data scattered across dozens of places, nobody could really see how the business was doing.

Why it worked: the map first, then the code

We didn't start from technology, but from the process. We mapped every flow one by one: where each lead comes from, which emails it receives and when, what happens at each stage, who does what. That map became the exact specification to rebuild.

That's why the platform isn't generic software bent into shape: it follows the real sales stages, the real team roles and the real commission rules. People who use it find their own work, just without the obstacles.

How long it took: under six months

In April, the analysis of all 54 tools. By July the new sales CRM was used by the whole team. In August, the complete email map; in September, email on their own infrastructure. Every month, one more piece in place and one less subscription.

How much effort for the team: the bare minimum

Each new system ran alongside the old one until it worked on its own, and only then was the old one switched off. The team kept working as usual, and issues were fixed day by day.

Today the sales team sees in every profile which emails a lead opened and which videos they watched. Leadership sees revenue, trajectory and team performance in real time. A segment or a new automation, which used to take hours, now takes minutes.
