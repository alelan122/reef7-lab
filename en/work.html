Sales CRM

A CRM built around a solar company's sales process, from first contact to installation.

A pipeline with the real stages, from site survey to signed contract, a profile for every customer with AI classification and briefing, objections captured from calls and suggested content to send after the survey. At the top, cash tracking against target with a traffic light and automatic diagnosis; every payment updates the leaderboard and tiered commissions on its own.

  • Kanban and list pipeline, with a required reason for lost deals
  • Lead profile with AI classification and briefing
  • Cash, trajectory and "why are we behind?" automatically
  • Leaderboard and commissions calculated on cash collected
