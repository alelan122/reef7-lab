My Daily Peak

An app that stays with customers after they buy and helps them reach their goal.

Customers choose what they want to improve and get a day-by-day plan: a check-in that takes seconds, one small habit and progress that's always visible. The app lives inside the store, is reserved for customers and reminds them to reorder at the right moment.

  • A plan tailored to each customer's goal
  • Daily check-in in a few seconds
  • Progress and milestones week after week
  • Built into the Shopify theme, no external apps
